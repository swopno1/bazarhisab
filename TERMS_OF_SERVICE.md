## 2. Description of Service

Bazar Hisab is a local-first household grocery-expense tracker (Bangla and English) that lets you:

- Record expenses manually, by voice, or by photographing paper receipts.
- Organize shopping lists, budgets, categories, and history, with CSV export.
- Optionally back up and sync records through your Google account (Firebase), and extract receipt/voice data with AI assistance.

The free app is supported by Google ads. Cloud backup, sync, and AI extraction are optional; manual entry, lists, budgets, and history work fully offline.
